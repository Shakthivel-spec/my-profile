01About

I'm a Software Developer with 5+ years of professional experience building enterprise and e-governance applications — financial management and healthcare information systems, workflow and approval systems, MIS dashboards, and REST APIs. I work on both the backend and the full stack, using Laravel/PHP and Node.js on the server and React.js on the web, with database-driven workflows and role-based access control at the core of each system.

Most recently I developed Team22 HRMS, a Human Resource Management System with a React.js web application and a React Native Android app, and handled its production deployment and Google Play release as Team22-HRMS. At In22 Labs I also deliver Laravel systems for financial management and healthcare administration.

I extend that backend foundation into Python/FastAPI services for AI and LLM integration — Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI chat, and natural-language database querying. Before In22 Labs, I spent over four years at Divinfosys building full-stack applications with Laravel and React and leading a development team across multiple client projects.
